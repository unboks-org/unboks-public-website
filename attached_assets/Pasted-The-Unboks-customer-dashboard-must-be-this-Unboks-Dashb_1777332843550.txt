The Unboks customer dashboard must be this:

Unboks Dashboard = Gmail-style AI Inbox Control Center

Not a SaaS analytics dashboard.
Not a setup wizard.
Not a social media publishing tool.
Not a booking dashboard.

It is the place where the customer manages this question:

“What does the AI need from me today?”

The client has already been onboarded by Unboks. Meta/WhatsApp/Zernio connection is already handled live by Unboks. The customer logs in after setup.

⸻

1. Main dashboard structure

The main navigation should be minimal:

Inbox
Escalations
Channels
Settings

That is enough.

Everything else goes inside Settings or behind advanced views.

No big menu jungle. No fake productivity dashboard. No random cards.

⸻

2. Inbox — the core product

The Inbox must be a real Gmail-style inbox.

This is the main screen. It should feel familiar immediately.

Inbox layout

Desktop:

Left sidebar/nav
Top search/filter bar
Conversation/message list
Conversation detail pane

Like Gmail:

* rows
* checkboxes
* sender name
* subject/message preview
* timestamp
* unread bold text
* labels/chips
* channel icon
* star/pin optional
* archive
* mark read/unread
* bulk select
* search
* filters

Inbox row should show

Each row should show:

Checkbox
Channel icon
Customer name
Message preview
Status label
Escalation label if needed
Last activity time
Unread indicator

Example row:

☐  WhatsApp  Maria Gomez   “Do you have availability tomorrow?”   AI replied   2m ago
☐  Instagram David K.      “What are your prices?”                 Escalated   6m ago
☐  Email     Sarah Ltd     “Can we schedule a demo?”               Human needed  12m ago

Inbox filters

At the top:

All
Unread
Escalated
AI Handled
Human Needed
Archived

Channel filters:

WhatsApp
Email
Instagram
Facebook
X
TikTok

The customer should be able to select one or multiple channels.

Bulk actions

When rows are selected:

Archive
Mark read
Mark unread
Assign
Resolve

Do not hide these behind hover only. They must be visible and obvious.

Conversation detail pane

When clicking a conversation, the right/main pane opens the conversation.

It should show:

* customer name
* channel
* status
* full message history
* AI replies
* human replies
* escalation reason
* internal notes
* reply box
* actions

Important: no page jump. The inbox list should stay stable.

3. Escalations — the most important daily page

Escalations are where the customer spends most of the time.

The Escalations page should be a focused queue:

“These are the conversations where the AI needs human help.”

Two escalation types

Soft escalation

The AI asks the human/operator for help.

Flow:

Customer asks something
AI is unsure
AI asks human internally
Human replies to AI
AI replies to customer

The customer may not know a human helped.

Dashboard UI should make this clear:

Soft escalation
AI needs guidance
Reply to AI

Buttons:

Answer AI
Edit suggested reply
Send AI Reply
Mark resolved

Example:

Customer: “Can I get the Valentine’s discount tomorrow?”
AI question to human: “I don’t know if the Valentine’s discount is valid tomorrow. Please confirm.”
Human answer: “Yes, valid until Sunday.”
AI sends to customer: “Yes, the Valentine’s offer is valid until Sunday.”

Hard escalation

The AI stops and hands over.

Flow:

Customer asks something sensitive/complex
AI hands over to human
Human replies directly to customer
AI stays silent

Dashboard UI should show:

Hard escalation
Human must reply directly
AI paused

Buttons:

Take over
Reply to customer
Mark resolved
Hand back to AI
Archive

Example hard escalation cases:

* angry customer
* refund request
* legal issue
* payment issue
* complaint
* VIP lead
* unclear request
* human specifically requested
* anything outside allowed rules

⸻

4. The dashboard homepage

I would not create a classic “Overview” dashboard as the main page.

When the customer logs in, they should land on:

/dashboard → Inbox

At the top of Inbox, you can show a small daily status bar:

12 unread   4 escalations   89 AI-handled today   2 channels need attention

That is enough.

No giant charts. No useless stat cards.

⸻

5. Channels page

The Channels page should be simple status, not setup-heavy.

Remember: Unboks does onboarding and connection live.

The customer should only see:

WhatsApp       Connected
Instagram      Connected / Not connected
Facebook       Connected / Not connected
Email          Connected / Not connected
X              Coming soon / Not connected
TikTok         Coming soon / Not connected

Each channel card can show:

* status
* connected account/name
* last message received
* health status
* reconnect needed warning

But do not expose Zernio details.

No “connect Meta yourself” wizard for now.

If a channel needs action:

Needs attention — contact Unboks support

or

Schedule reconnection call

⸻

6. Settings page

Settings should contain the less-daily things.

Main Settings sections:

Business Info
Source of Truth
Rules
Team Members
Notifications
Language
Advanced

Business Info

Simple editable info:

* business name
* website
* phone
* email
* address
* opening hours
* languages
* short business description

Source of Truth

This should not be a big main menu item.

It lives inside Settings.

Purpose:

“Add information the AI is allowed to use.”

Customers should be trained to add updates whenever something changes.

They can upload:

* PDFs
* menus
* price lists
* service sheets
* screenshots
* images
* policies
* FAQs
* Word docs
* text notes

They can add quick updates:

Special offer this weekend
Holiday opening hours
Valentine’s Day promo
Temporary closure
New price
New service
Sold out product
Staff unavailable
Policy change

The UI should be extremely simple:

Add update
Upload file
View uploaded knowledge

Each item should show:

Title
Type
Date added
Status: Active / Expired / Draft
Used by AI: Yes/No

For time-sensitive info, allow expiry date:

Valentine’s Day promo
Active from Feb 10 to Feb 15

This prevents old promos from being used forever.

Rules

Rules define AI behavior.

Sections:

AI may answer automatically when...
AI must soft escalate when...
AI must hard escalate when...
AI must never answer...
Tone of voice
Languages
Human handoff rules

Examples:

AI may answer FAQs, opening hours, pricing, service descriptions.
AI must soft escalate if it lacks information.
AI must hard escalate for complaints, refunds, legal issues, payment problems.
AI must never invent prices or promises.
AI must never say a human confirmed something unless true.

Team Members

Who can access the dashboard:

* owner
* manager
* operator
* read-only

Notifications

Where escalation alerts go:

* email
* WhatsApp
* dashboard only
* mobile app later

Advanced

Hide complicated stuff here.

Possible advanced items:

* AI confidence threshold
* internal prompt settings
* dry run mode
* debug logs
* export data
* API/webhook info

Most customers should never touch this.

⸻

7. Reply experience

The reply box is critical.

For each conversation, the operator should understand who they are replying to:

In normal conversation

Reply to customer

In soft escalation

Answer AI

Because the human is not directly replying to the customer. They are giving information to the AI.

In hard escalation

Reply directly to customer

Because AI has handed over.

This must be visually obvious. No confusion.

⸻

8. Status labels

Every conversation should have one clear status.

Examples:

AI handled
Unread
Soft escalation
Hard escalation
Human active
Resolved
Archived
AI paused
Needs attention

Color coding helps, but labels must be readable without relying only on color.

⸻

9. Customer mental model

The customer should understand the system like this:

The AI answers normal repetitive questions.
When the AI is unsure, it asks me.
When the situation is serious, it hands over to me.
I can update what the AI knows in Settings.

That is the whole product.

⸻

12. Mobile version later

The mobile app should not try to copy the whole dashboard.

Mobile should focus on:

Escalations
Reply
Resolve
Hand back to AI

Mobile purpose:

“AI needs help. Human answers fast.”

No heavy settings. No full knowledge management. No big analytics.

⸻

13. Design standard

The dashboard should feel:

Gmail familiar
WhatsApp simple
Enterprise clean
Fast
Calm
No clutter
No jumping
No overlapping
Readable

Not “cool for the sake of cool.”

Premium means:

* spacing is clean
* text is readable
* actions are obvious
* status is clear
* nothing moves unexpectedly
* mobile does not break
* dark mode has enough contrast

⸻

14. The one-sentence definition

The Unboks customer dashboard is a Gmail-style AI inbox where customers review conversations, handle soft and hard escalations, monitor connected channels, and update the AI’s source of truth through Settings.
