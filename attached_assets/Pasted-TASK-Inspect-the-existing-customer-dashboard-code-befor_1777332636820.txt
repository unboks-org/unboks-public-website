Context:
We are building the Unboks.org customer dashboard.

Unboks helps businesses automatically answer repetitive WhatsApp messages, emails, and social media DMs. The dashboard is mainly for reviewing conversations and handling escalations.

This dashboard is NOT:
- a Meta onboarding wizard
- a Zernio setup screen
- a social media publishing dashboard
- a booking/capacity dashboard
- an internal Unboks admin panel

Customer onboarding happens before dashboard access:
1. Client contacts Unboks.
2. Unboks speaks with the client and agrees to proceed.
3. Unboks does an intake conversation.
4. Unboks helps connect Meta/WhatsApp/social channels live by Zoom or in person.
5. Unboks configures the client.
6. Client receives login credentials by email.
7. Client logs into the dashboard.

Therefore:
Do not build customer self-service Meta connection UI.
Do not expose Zernio to customers.
Do not make Source of Truth a main navigation item.

Desired customer dashboard main navigation:
- Inbox
- Escalations
- Channels
- Settings

Settings should contain:
- Business Info
- Source of Truth / Knowledge Uploads
- Rules
- Team Members
- Notifications
- Language
- Advanced

Source of Truth / Knowledge Uploads:
This should be a simple Settings area where clients can upload files and add comments/updates for the AI to use.

Examples:
- PDFs
- images/screenshots
- menus
- pricing sheets
- service lists
- policies
- FAQs
- holiday schedules
- temporary opening hours
- special offers
- Valentine’s Day promotions
- tone instructions
- escalation instructions
- forbidden answers

The goal is to train customers that they can always add new information for the AI to use.

Main dashboard priority:
Escalations.

There are two escalation types:

1. Soft escalation
The AI/agent asks the operator/human a question.
The human replies to the AI/agent.
The AI/agent then replies to the customer.
The customer may not know a human helped behind the scenes.

2. Hard escalation
The AI/agent immediately hands over the conversation.
A human replies directly to the customer.
The AI/agent stops being involved until the conversation is resolved or handed back.
